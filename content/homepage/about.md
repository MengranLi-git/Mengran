I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Oxford. My research develops causal inference methodology for rare and extreme events, spanning causal discovery, extreme treatment effects, and climate event attribution. By combining causal inference with extreme value theory, I aim to support reliable decision-making in high-impact settings such as climate risk.
